# Objections

Each reply cites a promise-ledger row, or it is labelled an assumption. These are not testimonials. Readers are `docs/STRATEGY.md` section 6 and the Phase 1 checklist. There is no API and no schema beyond this list. The user said: "Audit phase 1 strictly. If not complete or has any issue, please fix it and correct it."

1. You will import our Tally company.
Reply: no. There is no Tally company reader (L39). The move is a spreadsheet import, opening invoices, opening stock, and a chart of accounts (L16, L26, L28, L29). Whether that is enough for a given firm is what the Process Audit is for (L47, assumption).

2. e-Invoice and e-way bill come off the sales order.
Reply: no. The e-invoice is generated from the sales invoice (L34). The e-way bill is generated from the delivery note, invoice, or stock documents named in L35, not from the sales order.

3. You file GSTR-3B on the GST portal for us.
Reply: no. The product prepares GSTR-3B and downloads JSON or Excel (L37). Portal save, submit, and file are not a claim.

4. The owner can see stock, sales, and dues on a phone app.
Reply: no. There is no owner phone app for stock, sales, and receivables (L40).

5. You collect UPI.
Reply: no. UPI is not a claim (L41).

6. The hosting includes automatic backups.
Reply: no. Automatic backups are not a product claim (L42). Hosting itself is not in this offer.

7. PF and ESI are deducted on the salary slip.
Reply: no. A salary slip can be printed (L31). Deducting PF or ESI on that slip is not a claim (L43).

8. What does it cost?
Reply: the three paid offers use `{{STARTER_PRICE}}`, `{{GROWTH_PRICE}}`, and `{{MANAGED_PRICE}}`. The logic is `{{DAY_RATE}}` times the days in the statement of work. No rupee amount is set (L49, placeholder). The Process Audit is free. It has no price token.

9. Show me GST on a live company.
Reply: not until the owner names an India Compliance revision that boots on a clean company (L45). The files exist. A live portal demo is not offered.

10. You are the same as any other implementation shop.
Reply, labelled a recommendation, not a measured difference: the work is the books, stock, and GST paperwork the firm already has to file, with the refusals written in the scope before the build starts. A packaged product sells a licence. A generic shop often sells days without that written refusal list. Whether this reason scales past a two-person team is an assumption (L47). The test is in STRATEGY section 11.

11. The free audit is a trick to start a build.
Reply: the audit includes no build, no data move, and no price (L47, assumption). It qualifies whether the first work is inside L01 to L38 and outside L39 to L45. The two-person capacity ceiling is an assumption. The test is to stop booking when both people are already in audits.

12. If we leave, do we keep our data?
Reply: records can be exported (L27). The exit term on each paid offer says the firm keeps the export. We do not keep a copy of their books after handover unless they ask in writing.
