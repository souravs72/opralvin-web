## Who it is for

A trading firm with more than one godown, still run by the owner and one accountant, inside the band the owner stated. This is a calling order, not a count of demand (L46).

If the firm needs a phone app for stock and dues, a Tally company reader, or a live GST portal demonstration, this is the wrong product this year (L39, L40, L45).
